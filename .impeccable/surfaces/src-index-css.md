## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one illustrated field journal read in two lights — day pages on warm ivory paper, night pages under a near-black galaxy sky — never a dashboard with an inverted palette bolted on.

OWN-WORLD: Light: ivory/parchment ground (#f6f1e4), warm-ink text (#211b14), hairline borders in warm charcoal at low opacity, Prussian-blue accent (#2f4d8a), brass/ochre secondary (#a9762c). Dark: near-black navy ground (#0a0d18), translucent glass panels, hairline borders in white at low opacity, lifted periwinkle accent (#7d93e8), muted brass secondary (#d9b872). Fraunces serif for display headlines, Inter for body/UI, IBM Plex Mono for uppercase kickers/labels. Hairline borders over shadow for definition; restrained corner radii (no rounded-2xl card language); pill shapes reserved for controls/badges only.

STORY: A visitor reads a considered, editorial-grade site in either theme; toggling day/night reveals the same craftsmanship in a different atmosphere, proving it is one authored world, not two skins.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Composition, spatial nav, and Motion transitions unchanged; only global surfaces, type, spacing, borders, and color are replaced consistently across Nav, Home, About, Projects, Connect.

FORM: brief-pinned direction, given whole (world, exclusions, preservation constraints) by the user; no concept roll run — a precisely specified request per new-work.md's own exemption.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
